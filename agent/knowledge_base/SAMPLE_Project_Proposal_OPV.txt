INDIAN COAST GUARD (ICG)
PROJECT PROPOSAL — OFFSHORE PATROL VESSEL CLASS (BATCH-IV)
DOCUMENT NO: ICG/PROJ/OPV-IV/2024
CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

1.1 Objective
The Indian Coast Guard proposes the acquisition of 8 (eight) Offshore Patrol Vessels (OPVs) under Batch-IV programme to replace aging Vishwast-class vessels and enhance maritime surveillance capability in the Indian Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) and approaches to island territories.

1.2 Strategic Need
(a) India's EEZ of 2.02 million sq km requires continuous surveillance for:
    - Anti-smuggling and counter-narcotics operations
    - Anti-piracy patrol
    - Search and Rescue (SAR) operations
    - Fisheries protection
    - Oil spill response
    - Coastal security operations
(b) Current fleet analysis indicates a shortfall of 12 OPVs by 2028.
(c) The Batch-IV OPV programme addresses this capability gap.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 Principal Dimensions
- Length Overall (LOA): 98.0 metres
- Length Between Perpendiculars (LBP): 90.0 metres
- Beam (moulded): 14.6 metres
- Depth (moulded): 7.5 metres
- Design Draught: 3.8 metres
- Full Load Displacement: 2,350 tonnes

2.2 Performance Requirements
- Maximum Speed: ≥ 25 knots at full load displacement
- Cruising Speed: 14 knots
- Range: ≥ 6,500 nautical miles at 14 knots
- Endurance: ≥ 25 days
- Seakeeping: Capable of operating in Sea State 5; survivable in Sea State 8

2.3 Propulsion System
- Configuration: CODAD (Combined Diesel And Diesel)
- Main Engines: 2 × medium-speed diesel engines, each rated ≥ 6,500 kW at 750 rpm
- Gearbox: 2 × reduction gearbox with PTO for shaft generator
- Propellers: 2 × controllable pitch propellers (CPP)
- Bow Thruster: 1 × 350 kW retractable bow thruster for harbour manoeuvring

2.4 Auxiliary Systems
- Diesel Generators: 3 × 850 kW @ 450V, 60 Hz
- Emergency Generator: 1 × 200 kW
- Fresh Water Generators: 2 × reverse osmosis units, each producing ≥ 10 tonnes/day
- Air Conditioning: Centralized chilled water system for full complement in tropical conditions (ambient 45°C, seawater 32°C)

2.5 Accommodation
- Total Complement: 14 officers + 106 sailors = 120 persons
- Officer cabins: Single berth for CO, XO, and HODs; double berth for remaining officers
- Sailors: 4-berth cabins with en-suite facilities
- Mess facilities: Officers' mess (seating 20), CPOs' mess (seating 30), sailors' mess (seating 60)
- Hospital: 2-bed sick bay with operating theatre capability

3. WEAPONS AND SENSORS

3.1 Weapon Systems
- 1 × 30 mm CRN 91 automatic gun (forward)
- 2 × 12.7 mm SRCG (Stabilized Remote Controlled Gun) (port and starboard)
- Provision for fitting 2 × shoulder-launched Surface-to-Air Missiles

3.2 Sensor Suite
- 3D Air/Surface Search Radar: Range ≥ 100 km
- Navigation Radar: 2 × (X-band and S-band)
- Electro-Optical Director (EOD): Day TV camera, thermal imager, laser range finder
- Automatic Identification System (AIS): Class A transponder
- Communication: HF, VHF, UHF, SATCOM (INMARSAT Fleet Broadband and VSAT)

3.3 Aviation Facilities
- Flight Deck: Capable of operating ALH Dhruv (10.5 tonnes MTOW)
- Helicopter Hangar: Enclosed hangar for 1 × ALH
- HELO Grid: RAS (Replenishment at Sea) capable
- Aviation fuel capacity: 5 tonnes JP-5

4. POLLUTION RESPONSE CAPABILITY

4.1 Oil Spill Response Equipment
- Containment Boom: 500 metres (inflatable, offshore type)
- Skimmer System: 1 × oleophilic disc skimmer (capacity 50 m³/hour)
- Recovered Oil Storage: 100 m³ dedicated storage tanks
- Dispersant Spray System: Ship-mounted spray arm and helicopter-mounted spray kit
- Monitoring: Oil-in-water monitor and infrared camera for oil detection

5. CONSTRUCTION AND DELIVERY SCHEDULE

5.1 Build Programme
- Shipyard: To be selected through competitive bidding (indigenous construction mandatory)
- Lead Ship: Delivery within 42 months from contract signing
- Follow-on Ships: Delivery at 6-month intervals
- All 8 ships: Complete delivery within 78 months

5.2 Quality Assurance
- Classification: IRS class with ICG oversight
- Shock qualification: Grade A for combat systems, Grade B for auxiliary systems
- Noise and vibration: Comply with ISO 6954:2000 for habitability
