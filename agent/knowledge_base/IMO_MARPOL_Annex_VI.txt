INTERNATIONAL MARITIME ORGANIZATION (IMO)
INTERNATIONAL CONVENTION FOR THE PREVENTION OF POLLUTION FROM SHIPS (MARPOL)
ANNEX VI: REGULATIONS FOR THE PREVENTION OF AIR POLLUTION FROM SHIPS

CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL

Regulation 1 — Application
1. This Annex applies to all ships, except where expressly provided otherwise.
2. The regulations in this Annex shall apply to all areas of the sea, including port areas.

Regulation 2 — Definitions
1. "Air pollutants" means substances emitted from ships into the atmosphere that may result in air quality degradation.
2. "Emission Control Area (ECA)" means a designated area where stricter emission standards apply.
3. "Sulphur content" means the proportion of sulphur contained in any fuel oil by weight.
4. "NOx Technical Code" means the Technical Code on Control of Emission of Nitrogen Oxides from Marine Diesel Engines.

CHAPTER 2 — SURVEY, CERTIFICATION AND MEANS OF CONTROL

Regulation 5 — Surveys
1. Every ship of 400 gross tonnage and above shall be subject to an initial survey before being put into service.
2. Subsequent surveys shall take place at intervals not exceeding 5 years.
3. An International Air Pollution Prevention (IAPP) Certificate shall be issued after a satisfactory survey.

CHAPTER 3 — REQUIREMENTS FOR CONTROL OF EMISSIONS FROM SHIPS

Regulation 12 — Ozone Depleting Substances
1. Any deliberate emissions of ozone depleting substances shall be prohibited.
2. Installations containing ozone depleting substances shall be prohibited on ships constructed after 19 May 2005.
3. Halon shall not be used on any ship.
4. New installations containing hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs) shall be prohibited on ships constructed after 1 January 2020.

Regulation 13 — Nitrogen Oxides (NOx)
1. Tier I: NOx emission limit of 17.0 g/kWh (engine speed < 130 rpm) for ships constructed from 1 January 2000 to 1 January 2011.
2. Tier II: NOx emission limit of 14.4 g/kWh (engine speed < 130 rpm) for ships constructed on or after 1 January 2011.
3. Tier III: NOx emission limit of 3.4 g/kWh (engine speed < 130 rpm) for ships operating in ECAs constructed on or after 1 January 2016.
4. For engines with speed 130 ≤ n < 2000 rpm: Tier I limit = 45 × n^(-0.2) g/kWh; Tier II limit = 44 × n^(-0.23) g/kWh; Tier III limit = 9 × n^(-0.2) g/kWh.

Regulation 14 — Sulphur Oxides (SOx) and Particulate Matter
1. Global sulphur cap: 0.50% m/m from 1 January 2020 (previously 3.50%).
2. ECA sulphur limit: 0.10% m/m from 1 January 2015.
3. Ships may use equivalent means of compliance (e.g., exhaust gas cleaning systems / scrubbers).
4. Fuel oil suppliers shall ensure the sulphur content is documented and does not exceed applicable limits.

Regulation 18 — Fuel Oil Quality
1. Fuel oil for combustion purposes delivered to ships shall comply with the applicable sulphur content requirements.
2. The supplier shall provide a bunker delivery note and a sample of the fuel oil delivered.
3. The bunker delivery note shall contain: (a) name and IMO number of the receiving ship; (b) port; (c) date of commencement of delivery; (d) name, address and telephone of supplier; (e) product name; (f) quantity; (g) density at 15°C; (h) sulphur content.

CHAPTER 4 — REGULATIONS ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY FOR SHIPS

Regulation 21 — Required EEDI (Energy Efficiency Design Index)
1. Every new ship shall achieve an EEDI not exceeding the reference line value × (1 - X/100).
2. Phase 0 (2013-2015): Reference line baseline.
3. Phase 1 (2015-2020): 10% reduction required.
4. Phase 2 (2020-2025): 20% reduction required.
5. Phase 3 (2025 onwards): 30% reduction required.
6. EEDI = (CO2 emission / Transport work) measured in grams of CO2 per tonne-nautical mile.

Regulation 22 — Required SEEMP (Ship Energy Efficiency Management Plan)
1. Each ship shall keep on board a Ship Energy Efficiency Management Plan (SEEMP).
2. The SEEMP shall include:
   (a) ship-specific measures to improve energy efficiency;
   (b) a method for voluntary reporting to IMO of fuel oil consumption data;
   (c) operational measures including weather routing and voyage optimization.

Regulation 22A — Collection and Reporting of Ship Fuel Oil Consumption Data
1. Each ship of 5,000 gross tonnage and above shall collect consumption data for each type of fuel oil used.
2. Data shall include: total fuel oil consumption, distance travelled, and hours underway.
3. The Administration shall issue a Statement of Compliance confirming data has been reported.

Regulation 23 — Required CII (Carbon Intensity Indicator)
1. From 1 January 2023, ships of 5,000 gross tonnage and above shall calculate their annual operational CII.
2. CII rating from A (best) to E (worst). Ships rated D for three consecutive years or E for any year shall submit a corrective action plan.
3. The annual operational CII is calculated as: CII = (Total CO2 emissions) / (DWT × Distance sailed).
