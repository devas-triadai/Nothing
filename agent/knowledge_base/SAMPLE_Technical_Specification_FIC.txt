INDIAN COAST GUARD (ICG)
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — FAST INTERCEPTOR CRAFT (FIC)
DOCUMENT NO: ICG/TECH/FIC/SPEC/2024
CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose
This document defines the technical specifications for Fast Interceptor Craft (FIC) for the Indian Coast Guard. The FIC is designed for high-speed interception, anti-smuggling operations, and close-shore patrol duties in the territorial waters and contiguous zone of India.

1.2 Operational Role
(a) High-speed pursuit and interception of suspect vessels
(b) Anti-smuggling and counter-narcotics operations
(c) Close-shore patrol and surveillance
(d) VIP escort duties
(e) Limited SAR role in coastal waters

2. PRINCIPAL CHARACTERISTICS

2.1 Dimensions
- Length Overall (LOA): 28.0 metres
- Beam (moulded): 5.8 metres
- Depth (moulded): 3.2 metres
- Design Draught: 1.5 metres
- Full Load Displacement: 110 tonnes
- Light Ship Displacement: 85 tonnes

2.2 Hull Form
- Type: Deep-V hull with strakes for spray deflection
- Material: Marine-grade aluminium alloy 5083-H321 (hull) and 6082-T6 (superstructure)
- Bottom deadrise angle: 18 degrees at transom; 50 degrees at bow
- Hull design to be validated through CFD analysis and model testing at NSTL

2.3 Performance
- Maximum Speed: ≥ 35 knots in Sea State 2 at full load displacement
- Cruising Speed: 15 knots
- Sprint Speed: ≥ 42 knots at light displacement
- Range: ≥ 500 nautical miles at 15 knots
- Endurance: ≥ 72 hours (3 days)
- Seakeeping: Operable in Sea State 4; survivable in Sea State 6

3. PROPULSION SYSTEM

3.1 Main Engines
- Type: 3 × high-speed marine diesel engines
- Power: Each rated ≥ 2,200 kW at 2,300 rpm (continuous rating)
- Certification: IMO Tier III NOx compliant
- Starting: Electric start with redundant battery banks

3.2 Propulsion
- Type: 3 × water-jet propulsion units
- Material: Stainless steel impellers
- Steering: Hydraulic bucket and deflector arrangement for each jet
- Reverse thrust: Full astern available through bucket deflection
- Intake: Flush-type intake with debris guard

3.3 Fuel System
- Fuel Type: F-76 (NATO) / HSD (Indian standard)
- Fuel Capacity: 12,000 litres (in 4 separate tanks for trim management)
- Fuel filtration: Dual-stage with 2-micron final filter and water separator

4. AUXILIARY SYSTEMS

4.1 Electrical
- Generators: 2 × 100 kW diesel generators (230/400V, 50 Hz, 3-phase)
- Battery Bank: 2 × 24V banks for engine starting and emergency power
- Shore Power: 440V shore connection with automatic changeover

4.2 Navigation Equipment
- Radar: 1 × X-band navigation radar with ARPA capability
- GPS: 2 × DGPS receivers with integrated plotter
- AIS: Class A transponder
- Echo Sounder: Digital with shallow water alarm
- Speed Log: Electromagnetic water speed log
- Compass: 1 × fibre-optic gyrocompass; 1 × magnetic compass

4.3 Communication
- VHF: 2 × fixed VHF-DSC sets; 2 × portable VHF
- HF: 1 × HF-DSC radio (150W)
- SATCOM: 1 × mini-M SATCOM terminal
- EPIRB: 1 × float-free 406 MHz
- SART: 2 × AIS-SART

5. WEAPONS

5.1 Primary Weapon
- 1 × 12.7 mm heavy machine gun on forward powered mount
- Stabilized mount with remote control from bridge
- Ammunition stowage: 2,000 rounds

5.2 Secondary Weapons
- 2 × 7.62 mm general purpose machine guns (port and starboard wing positions)
- Small arms: 6 × 5.56 mm assault rifles; 2 × 9 mm pistols
- Small arms locker: Secured, ventilated, humidity-controlled

6. CREW AND ACCOMMODATION

6.1 Complement
- Total: 2 officers + 18 sailors = 20 persons
- Bridge crew: 1 officer + 2 sailors per watch
- Engine room: Unmanned with bridge control (alarm monitoring)

6.2 Accommodation Standards
- Officer cabin: 1 × double berth cabin with en-suite head
- Sailors: 2 × 6-berth cabins + 1 × 6-berth cabin
- Mess/galley: Combined mess for 12 persons with pantry
- Air conditioning: Split-type units for all accommodation spaces

7. SAFETY EQUIPMENT

7.1 Life-Saving
- Liferafts: 2 × 12-person inflatable liferafts (HRU-fitted)
- Life jackets: 24 (20 + 20% spare)
- Immersion suits: 20
- Lifebuoys: 4 (2 with self-igniting lights, 2 with buoyant line)

7.2 Fire Fighting
- Fire detection: Addressable smoke/heat detection system
- Fixed system: FM-200 (HFC-227ea) in engine room
- Portable: 6 × CO2 (5 kg), 4 × DCP (9 kg), 2 × foam (9 litre)
- Fire pump: 1 × engine-driven + 1 × electric fire pump

8. CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS

8.1 Classification
- To be built under classification rules of Indian Register of Shipping (IRS)
- Classification notation: ✠100 A1 (HSC), ✠LMC, (UMS)
- Material testing: All structural aluminium to be certified with mill certificates
- Welding: Qualified welders per IRS/AWS D1.2 standards
- NDT: 100% radiography of critical joints; 20% of non-critical joints

8.2 Delivery Schedule
- First vessel: 24 months from contract
- Subsequent vessels: 4-month intervals
- Total programme (8 vessels): 52 months
