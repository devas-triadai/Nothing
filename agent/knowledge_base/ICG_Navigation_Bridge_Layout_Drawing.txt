INDIAN COAST GUARD (ICG)
NAVIGATION BRIDGE LAYOUT DRAWING — OFFSHORE PATROL VESSEL (OPV)
DRAWING NO: ICG/DRAW/OPV/BRIDGE/004/2024
CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED

1. DRAWING OVERVIEW

1.1 Purpose
This document defines the layout, equipment arrangement, console dimensions, and sight-line requirements for the Navigation Bridge of the Indian Coast Guard Offshore Patrol Vessel (OPV). It governs the placement of all navigation, communication, and ship-control equipment on the bridge deck.

1.2 Applicable Standards
- SOLAS Chapter V — Safety of Navigation
- IMO Resolution MSC.191(79) — Performance Standards for Integrated Bridge Systems
- IMO Resolution A.1047(27) — Principles of Minimum Safe Manning
- IRS Rules Part 4 Chapter 5 — Navigation Equipment
- IEC 60945 — Maritime navigation equipment (environmental protection)

2. BRIDGE DECK PLAN (TOP VIEW)

2.1 Overall Bridge Dimensions
- Bridge total length (fore-aft): 12.0 m (Frame 55 to Frame 75)
- Bridge total breadth: 16.2 m (including both wings)
- Bridge interior clear width: 13.0 m (excluding wing enclosures)
- Bridge wing length (each side): 2.8 m (overhanging ship's side by 1.5 m)
- Deckhead height (inside): 2.7 m
- Window sill height from bridge deck: 1.0 m
- Window top height from bridge deck: 2.3 m (1.3 m glass panel height)

2.2 Equipment Zone Layout (Plan View, from aft to forward)

AFT ZONE (aft bulkhead area):
- Chart table (1.2 m × 0.9 m, sloped top, illuminated): Port-aft corner
- ECDIS workstation 1 (primary): Port side, 0.8 m from chart table
- ECDIS workstation 2 (backup): Starboard side, adjacent to ECDIS 1 mirror position
- Radio operator's console (0.9 m × 0.6 m): Starboard-aft corner
  - Contains: GMDSS panel, HF-DSC, Inmarsat-C, NAVTEX receiver
- Central walkway (1.2 m clear): Centreline fore-aft passage

MID ZONE (main operating area):
- Conning console (centreline, 3.0 m wide × 0.8 m deep, U-shape): Frame 68 position
  - Centre section: Autopilot control, helm (emergency manual wheel), rudder angle indicator
  - Port section: Engine order telegraph (×2 shafts), shaft RPM displays, bow thruster control
  - Starboard section: Navigation lighting panel, bridge lighting dimmer, anchor alarm display
- Radar console 1 (X-band, 25 kW): 1.2 m port of centreline, 1.0 m forward of conning console
- Radar console 2 (S-band, 30 kW): 1.2 m starboard of centreline, mirror position
- AIS display terminal: Between radar consoles on common pedestal
- VHF radio panels (×4 channels): Overhead rack above radar consoles

FORWARD ZONE (windows area):
- Magnetic compass binnacle: Centreline, 1.5 m aft of forward windows, clear of magnetic interference
- Speed log display: Integrated into conning console (centreline section)
- Echo sounder display: Integrated into conning console (port section)
- Gyrocompass repeater: Overhead, centreline
- Wind speed/direction display: Overhead, centreline

3. BRIDGE WING LAYOUT

3.1 Port Bridge Wing
- Wing conning position: Forward of wing, 0.5 m from ship's side extremity
  - Wing helm (emergency tiller input): Push-button or follow-up lever
  - Engine order telegraph (local wing repeater): 1 × lever unit
  - Rudder angle indicator: Circular dial, 0.3 m diameter
  - Magnetic compass (pelorus): Mounted on wing forward edge
- Navigation light status panel: Wing after bulkhead
- Searchlight control (1 × 2 kW Jabsco): Wing forward position, 360° traverse

3.2 Starboard Bridge Wing
- Mirror arrangement of port wing
- Additional: Man-overboard (MOB) alarm button (guarded, red)
- GMDSS EPIRB test activation panel (remote)

4. SIGHT-LINE REQUIREMENTS (per SOLAS V/22)

4.1 Forward Visibility
- Unobstructed view from bridge: ±10° on each side of the bow
- Blind sector forward of beam: Each blind sector ≤ 5°; total ≤ 10° in any 10° arc
- Distance of clear view from bridge to bow: ≤ 2 ship lengths (OPV: ≤ 210 m)
- Minimum view below horizontal from conning position: 2° above horizontal at 500 m ahead
- Conning eye height above bridge deck: 1.65 m (for 1.75 m person at station)
- Conning position height above waterline (full load): 9.8 m

4.2 Side and Aft Visibility
- Bridge wings must allow clear view along ship's side for mooring operations
- View aft: Via two radar screens showing AIS returns; no direct optical aft view required by regulation
- CCTV cameras (×4): Bow, stern, port amidships, starboard amidships — displays at chart table

5. CONSOLE DIMENSIONS AND ERGONOMICS

5.1 Conning Console Detail
- Total width: 3,000 mm
- Depth (reach): 750 mm (all controls within 600 mm reach)
- Console face angle: 15° sloped toward operator
- Control surface height from deck: 1,050 mm (working surface)
- Display screen height: 1,350–1,700 mm from deck (eye level range)
- Knee clearance: 650 mm height × 600 mm depth
- Seat provision: 2 × adjustable captain's chairs, 750 mm rail spacing

5.2 Radar Console Detail
- Console type: Standalone pedestal, 600 mm diameter circular base
- Display: 19-inch LCD, anti-reflection coating, night-vision compatible
- Range scales: 0.125 NM to 96 NM
- ARPA tracking: ≥ 40 targets simultaneously
- AIS overlay: Integrated, displaying Class A and B targets

5.3 Chart Table Detail
- Table top dimensions: 1,200 mm × 900 mm
- Illumination: Adjustable LED strips, red/white switchable (SOLAS compliance)
- Storage drawers: 3 × A0 chart drawers below tabletop
- ECDIS interface: USB/LAN connection for paper chart backup

6. COMMUNICATION EQUIPMENT LAYOUT

6.1 Fixed VHF Stations
- VHF Station 1 (CH 16 watch): Port radar console integrated handset
- VHF Station 2 (working channel): Starboard radar console integrated handset
- VHF Station 3 (GMDSS DSC): Radio console, dedicated DSC controller
- VHF Station 4 (internal comms/PA): Conning console centreline

6.2 HF Radio
- HF-DSC transceiver (150W): Radio console, starboard aft
- Antenna coupler: Mast top, remote-tuned, cable length 42 m
- HF frequencies programmed: 4, 8, 12, 16, 22 MHz (maritime distress and working)

6.3 Satellite Communications
- Mini-M SATCOM terminal: Radio console
  - Antenna: Above bridge deck at Frame 65 mast position (gimballed dome, 0.6 m diameter)
- Inmarsat-C terminal: Integrated in GMDSS panel
  - Data rate: 600 bps (distress messages and SafetyNET reception)

6.4 Internal Communications
- Engine room telegraph (primary): Conning console
- Engine room voice tube: Conning console (backup, gravity-type)
- Ship's PA (general alarm): Handset at conning console; power amplifier in radio room
- Combat Information Centre (CIC) intercom: Dedicated handset, conning console starboard

7. LIGHTING SPECIFICATION

7.1 Bridge Lighting Zones
- Navigational working area: Red lighting (≤ 1 lux) for night adaptation
- Chart table: White/red switchable, 500 lux white / 50 lux red
- Display screens: Individual brightness/contrast controls (0–100%)
- Emergency: Battery-backed fluorescent strip, 10 lux minimum

7.2 External Bridge Lights
- Steaming light (masthead): LED, 225° arc, 6 NM range
- Port navigation light: LED, red, 112.5° arc, 3 NM range
- Starboard navigation light: LED, green, 112.5° arc, 3 NM range
- Stern light: LED, white, 135° arc, 3 NM range
- Anchor light: LED, white, 360° arc, 3 NM range
- All navigation lights: Dual-element (automatic changeover on failure)

8. FIRE AND SAFETY EQUIPMENT ON BRIDGE

- Fire alarm panel (bridge repeater): Port aft bulkhead — displays all zone alarms
- Watertight door control panel: Starboard aft — open/close/indicate status all WT doors
- Fixed fire detection: 2 × smoke detectors, 1 × heat detector
- Fire extinguisher: 2 × 5 kg CO2 (bridge), 1 × 2 kg CO2 (radio room)
- Immersion suits storage: 4 suits in marked locker, port aft corner
- Emergency stop for funnel fans: Guarded button, bridge aft bulkhead

9. DRAWING NOTES

- Minimum aisle width between consoles: 900 mm (SOLAS requirement)
- All consoles finished in matte dark grey (Munsell N5 or equivalent) to minimise glare
- All cable runs behind console panels; minimum 25 mm conduit for data cables
- Console earth bonding: 10 mm² green/yellow cable to ship's earth bus
- Gyro compass master unit location: Gyro room below bridge at Frame 65
- All instruments to IEC 60945 electromagnetic compatibility standard
