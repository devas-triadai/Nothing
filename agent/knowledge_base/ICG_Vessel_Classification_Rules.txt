INDIAN COAST GUARD (ICG)
VESSEL CLASSIFICATION AND SURVEY RULES
ICG/TECH/CLASS/2024

CHAPTER 1 — CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK

Section 1.1 — Classification Societies
1. The Indian Coast Guard recognizes classification surveys conducted by Indian Register of Shipping (IRS) and other IACS member societies.
2. Classification surveys cover: hull structure, machinery, electrical installations, and safety equipment.
3. Ships shall maintain their class throughout their operational life.

Section 1.2 — Ship Categories for ICG
1. Offshore Patrol Vessel (OPV): 90-105 metres, 2,200-2,400 tonnes displacement.
2. Fast Patrol Vessel (FPV): 48-50 metres, 300-350 tonnes displacement.
3. Interceptor Boat (IB): 28-30 metres, 100-120 tonnes displacement.
4. Air Cushion Vehicle (ACV): For shallow water and amphibious operations.
5. Pollution Control Vessel (PCV): Equipped with oil spill recovery systems.
6. Dornier Aircraft: Maritime surveillance aircraft.
7. Advanced Light Helicopter (ALH): For SAR and coastal patrol operations.

CHAPTER 2 — SURVEY REQUIREMENTS

Section 2.1 — Periodic Survey Schedule
1. Annual Survey: Visual inspection of hull, machinery, and safety equipment conducted annually.
2. Intermediate Survey: Detailed inspection at 2.5 years including thickness measurement of hull plating.
3. Special Survey: Comprehensive survey at 5-year intervals including dry-docking and ultrasonic thickness gauging.
4. Bottom Survey: Hull bottom inspection in dry dock at intervals not exceeding 5 years (2.5 years for vessels over 15 years old).

Section 2.2 — Hull Survey Standards
1. Minimum hull plating thickness: Original as-built minus maximum allowable diminution.
2. Allowable diminution for general corrosion: 20% of original thickness for vessels up to 15 years; 15% for vessels over 15 years.
3. Localized corrosion: Maximum pitting depth not to exceed 25% of original thickness.
4. Critical structural members (keel, frames, longitudinals): Maximum diminution of 10%.

Section 2.3 — Machinery Survey Standards
1. Main engines: Crankshaft deflection readings shall be taken at each survey.
2. Maximum allowable crankshaft deflection: ±0.03 mm per 100 mm of stroke for 4-stroke engines; ±0.05 mm per 100 mm for 2-stroke engines.
3. Turbochargers: Complete overhaul at intervals recommended by manufacturer or 8,000 operating hours.
4. Steering gear: Complete functional test including emergency operation at each annual survey.

Section 2.4 — Electrical Survey Standards
1. Insulation resistance of all circuits shall be measured at each survey.
2. Minimum acceptable insulation resistance: 1 MΩ for circuits above 100V; 0.5 MΩ for circuits below 100V.
3. Emergency generator: Load test at each annual survey demonstrating 100% rated load for minimum 2 hours.
4. Navigation lights: All navigation lights and signals shall be tested at each survey.

CHAPTER 3 — DESIGN STANDARDS FOR NEW BUILDS

Section 3.1 — Structural Design
1. All new ICG vessels shall be designed to meet or exceed IRS classification rules.
2. Design loads shall account for North Indian Ocean sea state conditions up to Sea State 6.
3. Fatigue life calculation shall demonstrate minimum 25-year service life for primary structure.
4. Vibration analysis: Forced vibration levels shall not exceed 4 mm/s at 1x and 2x propeller blade passing frequency.

Section 3.2 — Machinery Selection Criteria
1. Main propulsion: Preference for combined diesel or gas turbine (CODOG/CODAD) for OPVs.
2. Speed requirements: OPV ≥ 23 knots; FPV ≥ 28 knots; IB ≥ 35 knots.
3. Range requirements: OPV ≥ 5,000 nautical miles at 14 knots; FPV ≥ 2,000 nm at 14 knots.
4. Endurance: OPV ≥ 20 days; FPV ≥ 7 days; IB ≥ 3 days.
