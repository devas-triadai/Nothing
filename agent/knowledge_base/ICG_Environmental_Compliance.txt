INDIAN COAST GUARD (ICG)
ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE REGULATIONS
ICG/ENV/COMP/2024

CHAPTER 1 — COASTAL ZONE MANAGEMENT

Section 1.1 — Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) Framework
1. The ICG enforces the Coastal Regulation Zone Notification issued under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.
2. CRZ categories: CRZ-I (ecologically sensitive), CRZ-II (developed urban areas), CRZ-III (rural areas), CRZ-IV (water area from LTL to 12 nm).
3. No new construction is permitted within 200 metres of the High Tide Line (HTL) in CRZ-III areas.

Section 1.2 — Marine Protected Areas (MPAs)
1. The ICG monitors and enforces regulations in designated Marine Protected Areas.
2. Prohibited activities in MPAs: (a) anchoring except in emergencies; (b) discharge of any waste; (c) bottom trawling; (d) collection of marine organisms.
3. Buffer zone: A 1 km buffer zone around all MPAs shall be patrolled regularly.

CHAPTER 2 — SHIP-GENERATED WASTE MANAGEMENT

Section 2.1 — Garbage Management Plan
1. Every vessel of 100 gross tonnage and above and every vessel certified to carry 15 or more persons shall carry a Garbage Management Plan.
2. Garbage categories under MARPOL Annex V: (A) plastics; (B) food waste; (C) domestic waste; (D) cooking oil; (E) incinerator ash; (F) operational waste; (G) cargo residues; (H) animal carcasses; (I) fishing gear.
3. Discharge of plastics into the sea is PROHIBITED at all times.
4. Discharge of food waste: Permitted when comminuted or ground, not less than 3 nautical miles from nearest land; permitted when not comminuted, not less than 12 nm from nearest land.

Section 2.2 — Sewage Discharge Standards
1. Ships shall not discharge sewage within 3 nm of the nearest land unless the ship has in operation an approved sewage treatment plant certified to meet the resolution MEPC.227(64) standard.
2. Between 3 nm and 12 nm: Sewage may be discharged if comminuted and disinfected using an approved system.
3. Beyond 12 nm: Untreated sewage may be discharged at a moderate rate while the ship is proceeding en route at not less than 4 knots.

Section 2.3 — Ballast Water Management
1. All ships shall comply with the International Convention for the Control and Management of Ships' Ballast Water and Sediments (BWM Convention).
2. From 2024, all ships shall meet the D-2 standard (performance standard): discharge less than 10 viable organisms per cubic metre (≥50 μm) and less than 10 viable organisms per millilitre (10-50 μm).
3. Ballast water exchange (D-1 standard): At least 200 nm from nearest land and in water at least 200 metres deep; achieving 95% volumetric exchange.

CHAPTER 3 — EMISSION MONITORING AND ENFORCEMENT

Section 3.1 — Sulphur Content Verification
1. ICG enforcement vessels shall carry portable sulphur analyzers for fuel sampling during inspections.
2. Sample collection procedure: (a) collect sample at fuel manifold connection; (b) seal and label with vessel name, date, and sampler's signature; (c) retain on-board sample for minimum 12 months.
3. Non-compliant vessels shall be reported to the Flag State and may be detained until compliant fuel is obtained.

Section 3.2 — Carbon Intensity Monitoring
1. The ICG tracks CII ratings of vessels calling at Indian ports.
2. Vessels with CII rating of D for 3 consecutive years or E for any year shall submit a corrective action plan.
3. Port State Control inspectors may verify SEEMP Part III compliance during inspections.

CHAPTER 4 — MARINE BIODIVERSITY PROTECTION

Section 4.1 — Endangered Species Protection
1. The ICG enforces the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 in territorial waters.
2. Protected marine species include: whale shark, dugong, sea turtles (all species), seahorses, and all cetaceans.
3. Penalty for harming protected species: Up to 7 years imprisonment and/or fine.

Section 4.2 — Anti-Poaching Operations
1. The ICG conducts anti-poaching patrols in the EEZ to protect marine biodiversity.
2. Unauthorized fishing by foreign vessels in the EEZ is prohibited under the Maritime Zones of India Act, 1981.
3. Fishing gear specifications: mesh size not less than 20 mm for trawl nets; gill nets not exceeding 2.5 km total length.
