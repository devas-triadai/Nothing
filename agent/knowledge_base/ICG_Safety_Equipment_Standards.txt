INDIAN COAST GUARD (ICG)
SAFETY EQUIPMENT STANDARDS
ICG/SAFETY/EQ/2024

CHAPTER 1 — LIFE-SAVING APPLIANCES

Section 1.1 — Lifeboat Requirements
1. Every Coast Guard vessel of 500 GT and above shall carry lifeboats with total capacity for 100% of persons on board.
2. Lifeboat types: (a) Totally enclosed lifeboats for OPVs; (b) Partially enclosed lifeboats for FPVs; (c) Rigid inflatable boats (RIBs) for interceptor boats.
3. Lifeboat capacity: Minimum 6 persons, maximum 150 persons. Each seat shall provide at least 430 mm width.
4. Launching arrangements: Free-fall or davit-launched. Davit-launched lifeboats shall be capable of being launched within 10 minutes.

Section 1.2 — Liferaft Requirements
1. Inflatable liferafts shall comply with SOLAS Chapter III and LSA Code requirements.
2. Capacity: Available for 100% of persons on board (in addition to lifeboats on passenger vessels).
3. Servicing intervals: Annual servicing at an approved service station.
4. Float-free arrangements: All liferafts shall be fitted with a hydrostatic release unit (HRU) for automatic deployment.

Section 1.3 — Personal Life-Saving Equipment
1. Life jackets: One for each person on board plus 10% additional for visitors/passengers.
2. Life jacket performance: Shall turn an unconscious person from face-down to face-up position within 5 seconds.
3. Immersion suits: Required on all vessels operating in water temperatures below 15°C.
4. Thermal protective aids (TPAs): 10% of complement or 2 units, whichever is greater.

Section 1.4 — Visual and Audible Distress Signals
1. Minimum equipment per vessel: 12 parachute rockets, 6 hand flares, 4 buoyant smoke signals.
2. Parachute rockets: Shall reach minimum 300 metres height and burn for minimum 40 seconds.
3. Hand flares: Burn time minimum 1 minute, visible at 5 nautical miles.
4. Expiry: All pyrotechnics shall be replaced before expiry date (maximum 3 years from manufacture).

CHAPTER 2 — FIRE SAFETY EQUIPMENT

Section 2.1 — Structural Fire Protection
1. All vessels shall comply with SOLAS Chapter II-2 fire protection requirements.
2. Fire zones: Main vertical zones shall not exceed 40 metres in length.
3. Bulkhead and deck insulation: A-60 class divisions between machinery spaces and accommodation; A-15 between accommodation areas.
4. Escape routes: Two means of escape from all spaces; corridors minimum 900 mm width.

Section 2.2 — Fire Detection and Alarm Systems
1. All enclosed spaces shall be fitted with automatic fire detection systems.
2. Detector types: (a) Smoke detectors in accommodation and service spaces; (b) Heat detectors in galleys and laundries; (c) Flame detectors in machinery spaces.
3. Fire alarm system: Audible and visual alarms throughout the vessel; central alarm panel on the bridge.
4. Manual call points: Located at all exit points, maximum 20 metres apart in corridors.

Section 2.3 — Fixed Fire-Extinguishing Systems
1. Machinery spaces: CO2 or clean agent (HFC-227ea or FK-5-1-12) fixed system.
2. CO2 system capacity: Sufficient to provide 40% of the gross volume of the largest machinery space.
3. Clean agent systems: Concentration as per manufacturer's specification for Class B fires.
4. Water spray systems: In vehicle spaces, ro-ro spaces, and helicopter decks.
5. System activation: Remote operation from outside the protected space; audible pre-discharge alarm for minimum 20 seconds.

Section 2.4 — Portable Fire-Fighting Equipment
1. Fire extinguishers: Minimum 1 per 200 square metres of accommodation area.
2. Types: (a) 9-litre water for accommodation; (b) 9 kg CO2 for electrical spaces; (c) 9 kg dry chemical powder for machinery spaces.
3. Fire hoses: Minimum 2 fire hoses available for any part of the vessel; nozzle capable of producing jet and spray patterns.
4. Fireman's outfits: Minimum 2 sets on vessels over 500 GT; 4 sets on vessels over 2,000 GT. Each set includes breathing apparatus (minimum 30-minute capacity), fireproof suit, helmet, boots, and axe.

CHAPTER 3 — NAVIGATION SAFETY EQUIPMENT

Section 3.1 — Navigation Equipment Requirements
1. Radar: Two independent radar systems (X-band and S-band) on vessels over 500 GT.
2. AIS: Class A transponder on all vessels over 300 GT.
3. GMDSS: Equipment as per the vessel's sea area of operation (A1, A2, A3, or A4).
4. ECDIS: Electronic Chart Display and Information System with approved chart data.
5. Echo sounder: Capable of measuring depth to at least 200 metres.
6. Speed log: Dual-axis water speed log on vessels over 500 GT.
7. Gyro compass: On all vessels over 500 GT, with minimum 2 repeaters.
8. Magnetic compass: Maintained as backup on all vessels.

Section 3.2 — Communication Equipment
1. VHF: Minimum 3 VHF sets (bridge, emergency, portable).
2. MF/HF: On vessels operating beyond A1 sea areas.
3. INMARSAT: Fleet Broadband terminal on OPVs and larger vessels.
4. EPIRB: 406 MHz COSPAS-SARSAT EPIRB, float-free mounted.
5. SART: 2 radar transponder SARTs or AIS-SARTs.
6. NAVTEX: For reception of maritime safety information.
